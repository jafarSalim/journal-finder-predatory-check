Predatory Check · warning signs, with the reason for each

FinderCheck a journal

Look for yourself

  1. Do you know the journal? Have you or your colleagues read it, cited it or been asked to review for it?
  2. Can you find the publisher? A real postal address, named editors with institutional pages, a working contact.
  3. Is it really indexed where it says? Check DOAJ for open-access journals and the indexes directly, not the logos on its site.
  4. Are the fees clear before you submit? And is peer review described, with a realistic timeline?
  5. Is the web address the journal's own? Hijacked clones copy a real journal's name and ISSN on a look-alike domain.
